Dear Aya
written in stone, sealed in gold
My dear,

 

I've been wondering for weeks now about how I'm going to write this. I mean, how can I summarise a whole year in a few short paragraphs? How can I best describe what you did to me this past year?

 

In my attempts to convey my thoughts, I've gone back and read all our conversations, hoping that somewhere between the words I had already written, I would finally discover the perfect way to tell you how much I love you.

 

But then I realised something. It was the past me that fell in love with the past you, and as much as I loved those days, I love today even more with you. I love waking up every morning to your smile, and going to bed at night to your warmth. I love the memories we've made for each other, the joys and sorrows we've shared with each other, and the happiness in calling you mine, and the comfort in being yours.

 

You are my favourite story, the quietest cathedral my heart has ever known, and the certainty I never knew I needed. Whatever tomorrow asks of us, I will meet it the same way I met today, with my hand reaching for yours.

 

My love, my life, my pearl, my answered prayer, my comfort after every difficult day, my greatest blessing in this life, and, God willing, my future wife.

 

I pray that years from now we will read these words together while smiling at how little they managed to capture. Because no collection of letters, no language ever spoken, and no book ever written could fully contain what lives inside my heart for you.

 

If loving you were an act of worship, I would never grow tired of it.

 

If gratitude had a face, it would be mine every time I look at you.

 

If home had a heartbeat, it would sound exactly like your name.

 

Until my last breath, I will choose you.

 

In every season of life, I will choose you.

 

In every version of myself, I will choose you.

 

With all that I am,

 

and all that I will ever be.

 

Yours, now and forever,
 
Karim
